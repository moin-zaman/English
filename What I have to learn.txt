Common Types of Linkers

Addition (যোগ করার জন্য)
and, also, moreover, in addition, furthermore
I like traveling, and I enjoy meeting new people.


Contrast (বিরোধ/পার্থক্য দেখাতে)
but, however, although, yet, on the other hand, whereas
It was raining, but we still went to the beach.


Cause/Effect (কারণ ও ফলাফল)
because, so, therefore, thus, as a result
The bus was late, so we missed the first show.


Sequence/Order (ক্রম/সময়)
first, then, next, finally, afterwards, meanwhile
First, we packed our bags. Then, we left for the station.


Examples (উদাহরণ দেখাতে)
for example, such as, for instance, like, namely
Many cities are famous for food, for example, Bangkok.


Conclusion (উপসংহার টানতে)
in conclusion, to sum up, overall, in short, finally
In conclusion, traveling teaches us many life lessons.Common Types of Linkers
